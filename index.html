Tone generates pure waveforms on the fly — no files, no libraries. Pick a shape, drag the frequency, and listen to the math.

How it works

🎛️

Oscillators

A single OscillatorNode is created the moment you hit play, then shaped and connected to your speakers.

📉

Click-free gain

Volume fades are scheduled on the audio clock, so starting and stopping never produces a pop.

🧮

Live note math

Frequencies are converted to note names using the standard MIDI formula: 69 + 12·log₂(f/440).
